# One More Adventure, Amor

Personal anniversary narrative adventure for Anami from Guillermo. Offline Windows, Godot 4, GDScript, Compatibility renderer. The player always controls Anami. Target is 30–45 minutes; prioritize a complete shorter route before optional polish. Duration must be measured rather than inferred from dialogue count.

## Story contract

`data/story.json` contains Spanish dramatized dialogue, scene objectives, tasks and keepsakes. Dialogue is fictionalized; historical events follow the supplied memories. Do not assign a year to the UEES MUN. Use Anami socially and “amor” between the couple. Choices converge and cannot block progression.

Third-person compact reconstruction guided by actual screenshots: house/pets/fish/cat, sugar cane/iron/mob/crop farms and trading hall, accessible switch/resource puzzle, waterfall/cave and forgiving corrupted mobs. Corrupted End recalls defeating the dragon followed by Anami accidentally entering the void. Safe bridge/portal puzzle replaces precision platforming. Boss phases: telegraphed attacks/shadows, keepsake pedestals, final strike freeing Guillermo's crystal. Humorous affectionate reunion and portal home.
6. **Beach:** Return to 2D, unnamed sunset beach. Walk and sit; no enemies/puzzles. Reflect on unexpected meeting and memories through dramatized dialogue. Personal letter remains visibly editable until Guillermo supplies it. Conclude “Te amo, amor.” / “Felices 3 años. ❤️” / “21 de octubre de 2023 — 21 de octubre de 2026”; graceful fade, credits and menu.
