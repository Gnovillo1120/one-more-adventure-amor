# Chapters 1–2 overhaul

Branch: feature/chapter-1-2-overhaul. Started from a clean working tree; main and origin/main matched the existing base. Existing main characters, outfit portraits, chapters 3–6 and original references are preserved.

Navigation now uses scene-defined spawn/exit/objective positions and a 20×12 pixel feet rectangle, with four-pixel movement substeps. Ikura's exit is at (1080,550), outside its furniture. Required/optional interactions use the same 72-pixel distance for prompts and activation. Old blocked save positions are repaired to nearby walkable terrain. Scene IDs/indices and save version 1 remain compatible; optional objective, quiz and order state are additive fields.

MUN: accreditation, committee introduction, proposal review, grounded debate, eight NPC variants with idle/walking activity, papers/placards, optional delegates, six-question UN quiz with three score reactions and callbacks, foot incident, lunch seating, diploma and quiet goodbye. Ikura chapter: explorable Yacht Club, photos/chat, apologies/date planning, host/table/waiter/customers, six illustrated dishes with recoverable ordering, optional topics and anonymous school-drama activity. Early scenes do not use couple nicknames. Conversations are dramatized, not historical transcripts; no remembered statistic is invented.

Research links and trivia answers are embedded in data/story.json. Original character art is retained. Notification and applause are original synthesized sound cues, not recordings.
